# Sports on FAST Platforms — Competitive Landscape

## Tubi's Sports Position (STRONGEST among FAST)

- **Fox Sports partnership**: Only FAST platform with direct pipeline from major broadcast sports network
- **NFL Channel**: 3.94M unique viewers (30d, Databricks verified) but only 4.0 min avg session — brief tune-ins
- **Super Bowl LIX (Feb 2025)**: Fox simulcast on Tubi — watershed moment for free premium live sports
- **Content flow from Fox**: Studio shows, post-game analysis, sports docs, classic replays, Fox Sports-branded linear channels
- **Strategic model**: Free funnel — expose casual fans, drive toward Fox broadcast for live games

## Competitor Sports Offerings

| Platform | Live Sports | Key Sports Content | Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tubi** | Select events (Super Bowl) | NFL Channel, Fox Sports linear | Fox vertical integration |
| **Roku** | Some via partners | Sports Hub aggregation, FAST sports channels | Discovery layer, not rights holder |
| **Amazon Fire TV** | TNF on Prime (paid) | Sports row, free highlights channels | Drive Prime subs via sports |
| **Pluto TV** | Very limited | CBS Sports HQ, beIN Xtra, Stadium, NFL Channel | News/highlights/niche, no premium live |
| **Samsung TV Plus** | Limited | Stadium, beIN Xtra | Device-bundled FAST |
| **Xumo** | Limited | Some sports channels | Charter/Comcast backed |

## Roku Sports Hub

- Dedicated sports destination on Roku devices
- Aggregates live/upcoming sports across installed apps
- Surfaces scores, schedules, highlights + free FAST channels
- Carries: NFL Channel, Stadium, beIN Xtra, select MLS/NWSL/MLB
- **Not a rights play** — discovery/navigation layer
- Tubi H1 FY26 plan includes Sports Hub integration (entry point)

## NFL Free Streaming

- **NFL Channel**: Free ad-supported linear on Tubi, Pluto, Roku, Samsung TV Plus
- Content: NFL Films, game replays, classic games (NOT live regular-season)
- NFL+ free tier explored but live games generally require paid sub
- Christmas 2024 games on Netflix showed NFL willingness to experiment
- **Key**: NFL is the most FAST-friendly major league

## Key Takeaways for Tubi Strategy

1. **Tubi has the best sports position among FAST** — no one else has Fox Sports direct pipeline
2. **NFL Channel is high-reach but low-depth** (4 min sessions) — need deeper sports engagement
3. **Sports Hub integrations** (Roku, potentially others) = critical entry points to build
4. **Live premium sports on FAST is still rare** — Super Bowl was the exception, not the norm
5. **Competitor sports offerings are mostly shoulder content** — Tubi's Fox advantage is real and durable
6. **Sports drives platform-level engagement** — whoever wins sports discovery wins linear

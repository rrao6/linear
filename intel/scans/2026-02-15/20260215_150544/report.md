## Executive Summary

In this competitive intelligence brief, we analyze the current landscape of the free ad-supported streaming TV (FAST) market, focusing on Tubi's positioning and strategic opportunities. Key developments include the expansion of sports streaming content, which is enhancing Tubi's appeal to sports fans, and the emergence of cheaper streaming bundles, positioning Tubi as a cost-effective alternative. Additionally, Tubi's focus on romantic and family content is broadening its audience base. These trends and opportunities are critical for Tubi's growth and competitive positioning in the evolving streaming market.

## Critical Threats

1. **Cheaper Streaming Bundles by Competitors**
   - **What it is**: Competitors like YouTube TV are introducing cheaper bundles, including sports-focused packages, to attract cost-conscious consumers.
   - **Why it matters**: This trend could divert potential viewers from Tubi, despite its free model, as consumers may prefer bundled services with premium content.
   - **Recommended Action**: Tubi should emphasize its free offering and explore partnerships to offer exclusive content that differentiates it from paid services.

2. **Generative AI in Streaming**
   - **What it is**: The integration of generative AI in streaming services is becoming more prevalent, as seen with Cineverse's Matchpoint® Creative Labs.
   - **Why it matters**: AI-driven content creation and personalization can significantly enhance user experience and engagement, potentially putting Tubi at a disadvantage if not adopted.
   - **Recommended Action**: Invest in AI technologies to improve content recommendations and explore AI-driven content creation to stay competitive.

3. **Increased Competition for Sports Streaming Rights**
   - **What it is**: The demand for streaming sports events is rising, with more platforms vying for exclusive rights.
   - **Why it matters**: Securing sports content is crucial for attracting and retaining sports fans, a key audience segment for Tubi.
   - **Recommended Action**: Form strategic partnerships with sports leagues to secure exclusive streaming rights and enhance sports content offerings.

## Key Opportunities

1. **Leverage Sports Events Streaming**
   - **What it is**: Capitalizing on major sports events like the World Baseball Classic to increase engagement and attract sports fans.
   - **What Tubi should do**: Enhance marketing efforts around sports events, create exclusive sports-related content, and collaborate with sports influencers to promote Tubi's sports streaming capabilities.

2. **Expand True Crime and Horror Offerings**
   - **What it is**: Strengthening the True Crime and Horror content clusters to attract and retain viewers interested in these genres.
   - **What Tubi should do**: Acquire more exclusive titles, develop original content, and promote these genres through targeted marketing campaigns.

3. **Enhance Family and Kids Programming**
   - **What it is**: Positioning Tubi as a go-to platform for family and kids programming by expanding content offerings in this category.
   - **What Tubi should do**: Acquire popular family and kids shows, create a dedicated section for family-friendly content, and partner with educational content creators.

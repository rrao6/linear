# CTV Identity Matching Errors — $7.4B in Wasted Ad Spend

## Key Findings (Truthset "State of Data Accuracy" Report)

- **40% of CTV ad dollars** in open programmatic market are wasted = **$7.4B**
- IP-to-email match error rate: **84%**
- IP-to-mailing address match error rate: **87%**
- Email-to-mailing address match error rate: **49%**
- Demographic segment error rate: **55% average** across 25 categories

## Root Cause
Each step in the data supply chain (IP → household → user → attribute) introduces ~50% error. NAT bundling by ISPs groups 16+ households under one IP address.

## Revenue Data Points
- YouTube ad revenue Q4 2025: **$11.38B**
- YouTube TV sports tier: **$65/mo** (not $73/mo — may have changed)
- Fox ad revenue growth Q4 2025: **1% YoY**
- Snapchat DAU end 2025: **474M**

## Implications for Tubi Linear
1. **FAST ad monetization is directly impacted** — if 40% of CTV ad spend is wasted, advertisers may shift budget away from open programmatic toward direct/PMP deals
2. **Identity accuracy = competitive advantage** — FAST services with better user data (logged-in users, deterministic IDs) can command premium CPMs
3. **Fox ad revenue growing only 1%** — modest growth for Tubi's parent, may pressure monetization targets
4. **YouTube's $11.38B quarter** shows the scale gap between YouTube/Google and FAST services

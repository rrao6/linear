# FAST/Linear TV Market Landscape

> Last updated: 2026-02-15

## Market Structure

The FAST (Free Ad-Supported Streaming Television) market consists of three tiers:

### Tier 1: Platform-Integrated (Own the OS)
These services are built into the TV operating system and have massive distribution advantages.

|---|---|---|---|

## Key Market Dynamics

### 1. Platform Integration = Distribution Advantage
The data is clear: Amazon drives 29% of Tubi's linear TVT via its Live tab, despite Roku being the larger VOD platform. OEM-level integration is the most powerful distribution mechanism for linear content.

### 2. Channel Count Arms Race
Top services offer 441-700 channels. Tubi has 275 (180 available per user). The gap matters for guide-based discovery but doesn't directly correlate with TVT.

### 3. Live Sports = Top of Funnel
Super Bowl LIX and Liga MX on Tubi drove massive spikes. Amazon (Thursday Night Football), Roku (sports hub), and Peacock (NFL, Olympics) are all investing heavily.

### 4. Cannibalization Tension
Every FAST/Linear service struggles with the same question: does linear viewing grow the pie or just shift it from VOD? Tubi models this at 50% cannibalization.

### 5. Ad Fill Rate = Industry Problem
Linear ad fill rates lag VOD across the industry. Tubi's fill rate is low enough that channel partners have threatened to leave. This is an industry-wide structural challenge.

## Market Size Indicators

- CTV ad spend: growing 20%+ annually
- FAST revenue: estimated $6-8B market (2025)
- Cord-cutting: 60%+ of US households are cordless
- Average US household uses 4+ streaming services
- Free tier usage growing faster than paid tiers

## Tubi's Competitive Position

**Unique Strengths:**
- Fox partnership provides tentpole live events (NFL, World Cup, Liga MX)
- Strong VOD catalog creates engagement flywheel
- Data showing Linear first viewers have better retention
- True Crime genre dominance in FAST

**Critical Gaps:**
- No platform-level integration (pure app play)
- Channel count below top competitors
- Linear TVT in decline since On Now unpinning
- Poor linear ad fill rate
- Limited off-platform discovery mechanisms for linear

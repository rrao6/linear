## How to Use These Sources

1. **Daily scan**: Check top 5 industry news sites for FAST/Linear mentions
2. **Weekly deep dive**: Search for specific competitor updates
3. **Monthly**: Review Nielsen Gauge, app store changes, job postings
4. **On-demand**: When a specific competitive question arises, use targeted searches

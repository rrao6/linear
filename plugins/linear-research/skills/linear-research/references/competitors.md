## Competitive Tiers

Tubi competes at multiple levels. Not just direct FAST competitors — the real competition for linear eyeballs includes vMVPDs, platform-integrated FAST, and even SVOD with live offerings.

## YouTube TV — Deep Profile (Premier Indirect Competitor)

YouTube TV deserves special attention because it represents what a fully-realized linear streaming experience looks like.

### Why YouTube TV Matters to Tubi
- **8M+ subscribers** paying $73/mo = dominant vMVPD
- **Best-in-class UX**: Clean EPG, unlimited DVR, multiview, profiles
- **Full channel lineup**: All major broadcast networks + cable channels + sports
- **Sports dominance**: NFL Sunday Ticket (exclusive), NBA, MLB, NHL, college sports
- **Platform advantage**: Deep Google/YouTube integration, Android TV/Google TV preferred placement
- **YouTube integration**: Clips, highlights, recommendations flow between YouTube and YouTube TV

### YouTube TV Feature Set (What "Great Linear UX" Looks Like)
- Unlimited cloud DVR (9 months storage)
- Up to 6 accounts per household
- 3 simultaneous streams
- Multiview (watch 4 streams at once)
- Key plays / live stats overlay for sports
- Start from beginning on live shows
- Picture-in-picture
- Works on every major device

### What Tubi Can Learn from YouTube TV
1. **EPG quality matters**: YouTube TV's guide is fast, clean, personalized — Tubi's EPG/browse experience needs to match
2. **DVR-like features for FAST**: "Start from beginning" and catch-up are expected by vMVPD users
3. **Sports UX innovations**: Multiview, key plays, stats overlay — these set expectations
4. **Cross-content discovery**: YouTube's recommendation engine moves users between short-form and live — Tubi's VOD+Linear hybrid should do this

### Where Tubi Wins vs YouTube TV
- **Free**: $0 vs $73/mo — massive price advantage for casual viewers
- **True Crime content depth**: YouTube TV carries networks but not Tubi's curated true crime cluster
- **VOD library**: 50K+ titles in VOD catalog
- **Fox Sports content**: Some content flows to Tubi that doesn't go to YouTube TV

## Tubi Competitive Position — CORRECTED

**Tubi has ~340 linear channels** (not 275 as previously stated from strategy doc).

### Strengths
- Fox Corp ownership → exclusive Fox Sports pipeline (Super Bowl, NFL, World Cup, Liga MX)
- VOD+Linear hybrid model → Linear+VOD users watch 2.4x more than VOD-only (35.2 vs 14.5 hrs, verified)
- True Crime dominance → ION + Dateline + ION Mystery + Forensic Files cluster = 30%+ of entertainment TVT
- 340 channels and growing
- Strong brand recognition in AVOD/FAST
- International expansion (MX, LATAM, CA, UK planned)

### Weaknesses
- No platform/OS integration — pure app play on every device
- Amazon dependency — 31.5% of linear TVT from one platform's deeplinks
- Linear TVT declining since On Now unpinning (Sep 2024, -18%)
- Lower ad fill rate on linear vs VOD
- Channel count still below Amazon (700+), Samsung (300+ marketed), Roku (350+)
- EPG/browse experience not best-in-class vs YouTube TV standard

### Strategic Gaps
1. **Platform integration**: Every Tier 1 competitor has OS-level advantage Tubi can't match
2. **Sports depth**: NFL Channel has huge reach (3.94M viewers/30d) but shallow engagement (4 min avg)
3. **Discovery**: 49.8% of linear sessions have no page_source attribution — can't optimize what you can't measure
4. **vMVPD competition**: YouTube TV at $73/mo sets the bar for "great linear" — free FAST needs to close the UX gap
